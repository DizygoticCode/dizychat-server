Quick start for new users

DizyChat is designed to feel familiar in seconds. Pick a room, introduce yourself, and use the composer tools as your community gets moving.

Enter your name and room

Head to /login, choose a display name, then join a public room or paste the room name your host shared.

Set your vibe

Toggle theme, density, and sound notifications so the chat feels right on desktop or mobile before the room gets busy.

Share more than text

  • Attach files, images, clips, or push-to-talk voice notes.
  • Add GIFs, custom emoji, reactions, and soundboard hits.
  • Paste links for rich previews and inline media where supported.

Keep track of the good stuff

Reply in threads, star messages for yourself, watch pinned posts from hosts, and use search filters when history grows.
