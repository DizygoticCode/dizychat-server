Your community, without Big Tech in the middle.

DizyChat is a home-hosted real-time community platform. Rooms, accounts, history, uploads, moderation, and the core realtime stack run on infrastructure we control, while optional integrations stay scoped to the jobs they perform. Chat from the web, Android app, or iPhone/iPad Home Screen, then add calls, media, watch parties, radio, and soundboards without handing the room to a social platform.

Everything in one self-hosted room

Chat, media, calls, and community control on a stack you own.

DizyChat combines identity-aware rooms, persistent history, fail-closed upload scanning, rich media, notifications, realtime communication, and host-grade moderation without outsourcing the community itself to a third-party chat platform.

Identity-first onboarding

Sign in with a registered account or confirm a guest identity first; room selection unlocks only after identity is ready, and room passwords stay separate from account passwords.

Home-hosted core

Express, Socket.IO, MongoDB state, local upload quarantine and ClamAV, media processing, and self-hosted LiveKit run under project control.

Moderator superpowers

Announce, pin, purge, mute, block, ban, or silence straight from chat. Role-aware controls keep room authority on the server.

Web, PWA & native

Use DizyChat in the browser, install it to an iPhone/iPad Home Screen, sideload the signed Android app, or build the native Capacitor iOS path.

First visit?

Identity first. Room second.

DizyChat now keeps account or guest identity separate from room access, so browser password managers do not confuse account credentials with private-room passwords.

Choose your identity

Sign in with a registered DizyChat account or confirm a guest name. Room controls stay locked until this step is complete.

Choose or create a room

Pick a public room or enter the room name your host shared. Add a room password only when the room itself is private.

Share more than text

  • Attach scanned files, images, clips, or voice notes.
  • Add GIPHY media, custom emoji, reactions, and soundboard hits.
  • Paste links for rich previews and inline media where supported.

Stay connected

Use search, pins, stars, room notifications, LiveKit calls, watch parties, and the mobile/PWA clients without changing communities.

Bring the room home.

DizyChat keeps the core community stack on infrastructure we control: identity, rooms, message history, media safety, moderation, and realtime communication. External integrations can add features without becoming the foundation of the community.
